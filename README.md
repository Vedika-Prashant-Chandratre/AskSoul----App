    <h1>AskSoul - AI-Powered Mental Wellness & Life Guidance App</h1>

    <p><strong>AskSoul</strong> is an AI-powered mental wellness and life-guidance application designed to support individuals dealing with daily emotional, mental, and life-related challenges. The app focuses on providing clarity, emotional support, and non-judgmental guidance in an accessible and user-friendly manner.</p>

    <h2>Purpose & Vision</h2>
    <p>AskSoul was created to address the growing problem of <strong>loneliness, mental stress, overthinking</strong>, and the lack of safe spaces for emotional expression. The vision is to make mental well-being an everyday practice rather than a crisis response, helping users feel <strong>heard, understood, and guided</strong> at any moment.</p>

    <p>In today’s fast-paced life, we often carry mental and emotional burdens that have no outlet. Many of us feel <strong>lonely, anxious, or overwhelmed</strong>, with questions about life, relationships, and purpose that we don’t know who to ask. We live in a world full of noise, but genuine listening, guidance, and understanding are rare.</p>

    <p>This is the core reason I created <strong>AskSoul</strong>—an AI-powered mental wellness and guidance app designed to <strong>give voice to your thoughts and clarity to your mind</strong>. It provides a safe, non-judgmental space where users can:</p>

    <ul>
        <li>Express feelings honestly without fear of judgment</li>
        <li>Receive guidance that blends spiritual wisdom with logical reasoning</li>
        <li>Find companionship during moments of loneliness or stress</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Core Features</h2>
    <p><strong>AskSoul</strong> is built around three thoughtfully designed tabs:</p>

    <h3>1. Guidance</h3>
    <p>Ask life-related questions and receive answers in <strong>Spiritual</strong>, <strong>Logical</strong>, or <strong>Balanced</strong> modes, helping users gain clarity and emotional stability.</p>

    <h3>2. Play with Mind</h3>
    <p>Interactive quizzes, games, and motivational talks designed to reduce stress, refresh the mind, and gently assess mood. This makes mental well-being engaging and easy to approach.</p>

    <h3>3. Mood & Companion</h3>
    <p>A supportive AI that listens patiently and helps users understand and track their emotions, especially during times of overwhelm. It provides companionship and guidance when users need it most.</p>
